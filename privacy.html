# StoreFrame Studio — Privacy Policy ## What StoreFrame Studio collects **Your projects, screenshots and videos stay on your Mac.** They are never uploaded to us. There is no account, no sign-in, and no analytics or crash-reporting SDK in the app. Three things do leave your Mac, and only these. ### 1. Licence verification (only if you have purchased) To keep Pro unlocked and to honour refunds and cancellations, the app periodically checks with our licence server whether your purchase is still active. That check sends your App Store purchase record — the receipt and transaction identifier Apple issues for this app — together with a random identifier. The random identifier cannot be linked back to you; it exists so a licence stays with the Mac it was bought for. **Sent to:** our licence server (hosted in the EU), which asks Apple whether the purchase is currently valid. **Stored:** the purchase identifier, which product it is, and whether it is currently active. Kept while the purchase exists and for a short period afterwards, for refund handling. **Not stored:** your name, your email, your Apple Account, or anything about the screenshots and videos you make. ### 2. AI features (only if you turn them on, and only with your own key) The AI features — Auto design, captions and translation — are optional and off until you paste an API key of your own. When you use them, the app sends **the screenshots you select** and the text prompt **directly from your Mac to the provider you chose** (Anthropic or OpenAI). Nothing passes through our servers and we never see your key or your images. Your use is billed to your own provider account, under that provider's privacy policy. Blur and pixelate regions are part of the design, not part of the image file. The app will not run Auto design while they are present, precisely so it cannot send an unredacted original by accident. Your API key is stored in the macOS Keychain on your Mac. It is never transmitted anywhere except to the provider it belongs to. ### 3. Purchases Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. ## What we never do - No accounts, no sign-in, no profile. - No analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking of any kind. - No selling or sharing of data with anyone, ever. - No access to your location, contacts, photo library, camera or microphone. ## Your choices - Not purchasing means no licence check is ever made. - Not adding an AI key means nothing is ever sent to an AI provider. - Deleting the app removes the installation id and your stored key from your Mac. ## Children StoreFrame Studio is a tool for app developers and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children. ## Contact Questions or a data request: stoyan.d.hristov@icloud.com
