Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zen2Code ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the StoreFrame Studio application (the "App"). By using the App you agree to this policy.

The short version

We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. The App runs entirely on your device, requires no account, and works offline.

Information we do not collect

  • No accounts, names, emails, or passwords.
  • No analytics, tracking, telemetry, advertising identifiers, or usage profiling.
  • No location, contacts, or device identifiers.
  • We operate no servers that receive your content.

Your content stays on your device

Screenshots, images, videos, text, and projects you create or import are processed and stored locally on your device. They are never uploaded to us. They leave your device only when you choose to export, save, or share them, and then only to the destination you select.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by the platform's app store (Apple App Store or Microsoft Store), not by us. We never receive your payment card or billing details. The store may provide us with anonymized or aggregated sales information (for example, number of units sold) that does not identify you.

Optional AI features (your own key)

If you choose to use AI-assisted features, you provide your own API key for a third-party AI provider. Your key is stored securely on your device (in the operating system keychain) and is never transmitted to us. When you use those features, your request is sent directly from your device to that provider under their privacy policy and terms. We do not proxy, read, log, or store your key, prompts, or results. Using these features is entirely optional.

Third-party links and marks

The App references device models and platforms (such as iPhone, iPad, Pixel, Mac, and others) that are trademarks of their respective owners. Those references are for descriptive purposes and do not imply any endorsement or data sharing.

Children

The App is not directed to children and, in any case, collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes to this policy
