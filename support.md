# StoreFrame Studio — Support

StoreFrame Studio is a Mac app for designing App Store screenshots and app preview videos.

## Get help

Email **stoyan.d.hristov@icloud.com**

To get a useful answer first time, include:

- what you were doing when it happened,
- the app version (menu **StoreFrame Studio → About**),
- a screenshot, if the problem is visual.

We reply as quickly as we can, usually within a couple of days.

## Common questions

**My exports still have the watermark after buying Pro.**
Open **Unlock → Restore Purchases**. If it persists, check that the Apple Account signed in to the
App Store is the one that bought Pro.

**I subscribed on another Mac.**
Purchases follow your Apple Account. Sign in with the same account and press **Restore Purchases**.

**My subscription ended but the app still shows Pro.**
The app re-checks in the background. Switch to another app and back, and it revalidates immediately.

**Video export asks me to upgrade.**
Video export is a Pro feature. Image and carousel exports are free, with a watermark.

**Where are my projects stored?**
Only on your Mac: as `.storeframe` files you save yourself, plus an automatic local draft of your
last session. Nothing is uploaded.

**How do I cancel a subscription?**
Apple menu → System Settings → your name → Media & Purchases → Subscriptions, or from the App
Store app. Cancelling keeps Pro until the end of the paid period.
